Waves of thoughts come and go,
some big, some small.
Like the moon pulls the tides,
time pulls my thoughts.
In all these waves —
the frothy ones that kiss my feet,
the ones that let me surf and glide,
the tsunamis that shake my soul —
you are not in any of them.
Because waves rise and fall.
But you remain,
quiet and constant,
like the shore, silently standing by the sea.
