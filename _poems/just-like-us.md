Not all stars are meant to shine forever.
Not all colors were meant to be captured in timeless art.
Just like us.

A shooting star —
A silent wish whispered into the night,
Fading softly into the dark.
Just like us.

The colors on a butterfly's wings —
Brilliant, delicate,
Here for a moment, then carried away by the wind.
Just like us.

Some things are too beautiful to last,
Too wild to be held,
Too radiant to fade slowly.
Just like us.

Not all great moments are eternal.
But the rarest, the realest,
Even in their brevity,
Leave a warmth that never truly fades.
Just like us.
