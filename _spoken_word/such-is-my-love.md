Imagine —
Writing soulful poetry in a language no one speaks.
Cooking feasts no one will taste.
Performing for an empty room.
Such is my love for you.
It fills my world, but has no place in yours.
Still, I write.
Still, I cook.
Still, I dance.
Still, I love you.
Because love isn't always a duet.
Sometimes, it's a solo whispered into the void,
hoping the echo comes back
sounding like your name.
