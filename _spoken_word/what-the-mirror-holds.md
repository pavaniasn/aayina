---
title: "What the Mirror Holds"
date: 2026-06-04
sketch_prompt: "A woman standing before a cracked mirror, her reflection incomplete, soft morning light through a window"
---

I have been practicing softness
the way you practice a language
you were never taught at home —

stumbling over syllables,
forgetting the grammar of gentleness
midsentence,
starting over.

There is a mirror in my house
that shows me everything
I have not yet become.

I used to look away.

Now I stand in front of it
and say: *I know you.*
*I am still learning your name.*

The cracks are not damage.
They are how the light
finds more surface to touch.
