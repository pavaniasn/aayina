I never thought
that love — so full, so joyful —
could become a burden.
With nowhere to go,
nowhere to flow.
Oh, how long I've endured the pain,
not knowing how to let it go.
But then,
the Universe whispered.
"Look beyond him.
There are other places. Other ways.
And maybe — just maybe —
you could try giving some of that love
to yourself."
God, it's hard!
To believe you deserve the love
you keep begging from the world.
To give to yourself
what you so freely give to everyone else.
But until I master that,
until I learn how to hold myself in the same warmth,
I'll keep letting it spill
into the world around me.
One act, one heart at a time.
